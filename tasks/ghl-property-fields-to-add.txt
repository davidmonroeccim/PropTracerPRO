BEFORE YOU START, THREE THINGS THAT MATTER.

Do not use the Money type for any of these. GoHighLevel MONETORY fields cannot be
written at all. Ten different wire formats were tried against original_upb and
current_upb on a real record and every one was rejected, which is why those two
loan fields still do not land. Money values go in a Number field instead.

The yes/no fields are Single line, not Checkbox. That matches what the property
object already does for Owner Is Individual and Owner Out Of State, which write
the words Yes and No. Keeping one convention means the push writes them the same
way everywhere.

Assessed Value is the county assessment. It is not a market valuation and must
never be labelled as one. On every parcel measured it was identical to the
vendor's own estimate, and against real sale prices it ran anywhere from 7 to 59
percent of what the property actually sold for. Portfolio Assessed Value is the
same number added up across everything the owner holds, so it carries the same
caveat: it measures how much they own, not what it is worth.

NOTE ON FIVE OF THEM

Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Tax Delinquent Since, Days On Market and Listing Price were
empty on all four sample properties. Create them anyway. The sample is commercial
parcels in Ohio, California and Utah, and what a county publishes varies a lot by
county. An empty field is a coverage gap, not a broken one.
